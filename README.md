A duel of ember mages, played with cards on a table. Both flames start at 30.
Spend ember to play creatures and spells, strike with your creatures, and put
the rival flame out. Guards must fall first. Sparks chain: play two Spark
cards in one turn and the second hits harder.

Four suits, told apart by colour and shape: Flame (red), Stone (slate blue),
Thorn (green), Spark (gold). The rival AI plays a readable plan — it guards
when hurt, answers your biggest creature, and goes for your flame when it
smells a win — so you can read it and counter it.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html` in
desktop Chrome. No network request, no build step.
